# VisionAssist roadmap from user research

Ranked by severity × how many people raised it, plus weak ratings, late alerts and gaps between real testers and synthetic low-vision users.

| # | Fix | Severity | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Warn by time-to-collision while walking | high | 5 tester(s) asked for it |
| 2 | Say when the camera cannot see (glass, darkness) | high | 3 tester(s) asked for it |
| 3 | Speak alerts earlier (time-to-collision instead of a fixed distance) | high | 33% said alerts came too late |
| 4 | Lead answers with the nearest obstacle | medium | 3 tester(s) asked for it |
| 5 | Raise "Trust in obstacle warnings" | medium | average 3.1 / 5 |
| 6 | Validate with low-vision users: Trust in warnings | medium | testers: moderate (3.1/5); synthetic low-vision users: low until the system proves itself; want a cane-first fallback |
| 7 | Validate with low-vision users: Alert timing | medium | testers: a third say too late; synthetic low-vision users: most want earlier, vibration as well as speech |
