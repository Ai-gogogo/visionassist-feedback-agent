Survey title: VisionAssist demo feedback

Context for the respondents (put this at the top of the survey, in plain words): VisionAssist is a student prototype that uses two phone cameras and a laptop to help people with low vision. It speaks obstacle alerts such as "Caution, chair straight ahead, about 1 meter", and a local AI assistant answers questions like "What is in front of me?" or "Why did you warn me?". It is a research demo, not a mobility aid. The survey takes about 3 minutes and answers are used only for our class project.

What we want to learn: whether the spoken alerts are clear and timely, whether the AI answers are useful and trustworthy, which feature matters most, and what to fix before the final demo.

Questions (keep them in this order):
1. Did you see the VisionAssist demo live, on video, or not at all? (single choice: Live, On video, Not yet)
2. How clear were the spoken obstacle alerts? (rating 1 to 5, 1 = hard to understand, 5 = very clear)
3. Did the alerts come early enough to act on? (single choice: Too late, About right, Too early, Not sure)
4. How useful were the assistant's answers about the surroundings? (rating 1 to 5)
5. How much would you trust VisionAssist to warn you about an obstacle you could not see? (rating 1 to 5, 1 = not at all, 5 = completely)
6. Which feature matters most to you? (single choice: Spoken obstacle alerts, Distance in meters, Asking the assistant questions, Remembering my belongings, Reading signs and text)
7. What is the one thing we should fix or add before the final demo? (open text)

Each panel member has a "role" detail (teammate, classmate or other). Keep it so results can be broken down by role.
